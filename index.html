KONTEKSTCONTEXT

Raiffeisen grupacija je htjela klijentima omogućiti da podignu kredit u potpunosti online, za nekoliko minuta, umjesto dolaska u poslovnicu. Rješenje je građeno tako da radi istovremeno u više država grupacije.The Raiffeisen Group wanted to let customers take out a loan entirely online, in a matter of minutes, instead of coming into a branch. MOJA ULOGAMY ROLE

Radio sam na backend dijelu procesa izdavanja kredita — poslovna logika, servisi i integracije. Također sam učestvovao u migraciji mikroservisa na novije verzije .NET-a.I worked on the backend of the loan-origination process — business logic, services and integrations. IZAZOV & RJEŠENJECHALLENGE & SOLUTION

Cilj je bio potpuno „end-to-end“ iskustvo: klijent unese potrebne dokumente, nakon odobrenja potpiše ugovor, automatski mu se otvori novi račun i sredstva se isplate — bez odlaska u poslovnicu. Kako u tom trenutku u BiH digitalni potpis još nije bio dostupan, tok je isporučen kao „end-to-branch“ (potpis u poslovnici), dok je arhitektura ostala spremna za puni end-to-end proces. Sistem je građen na .NET-u i EF Core-u, sa SQL Server bazom i mikroservisnom arhitekturom.The goal was a fully end-to-end experience: the customer submits the required documents, signs the contract after approval, a new account is opened automatically and the funds are disbursed — with no branch visit. Since a digital signature was not yet available in Bosnia and Herzegovina at the time, the flow was delivered as “end-to-branch” (signing in the branch), while the architecture stayed ready for the full end-to-end process. The system was built on .NET and EF Core, with a SQL Server database and a microservice architecture.

REZULTATRESULT

Klijenti mogu dobiti kredit za samo nekoliko minuta, uz znatno manje papirologije i bez odlaska u poslovnicu. Migracija mikroservisa na novije verzije .NET-a poboljšala je performanse i održivost sistema.Customers can get a loan in just a few minutes, with far less paperwork and no branch visit. Migrating the microservices to newer .NET versions improved the system's performance and maintainability.

The multi-tenant model lets each tenant have an isolated portal and dashboard while sharing the same codebase.

REZULTATRESULT
